# The summary written back in chat

Moved out of `SKILL.md` to keep it lean; read it before writing the summary back.

## THE SUMMARY — written back in chat, every roll

The workbook is the deliverable, but nobody reads a diff. Close every roll with a
short written summary, in this order:

1. **One line:** period closed, roll mode, scope, and the filename of the undo copy.
2. **What the print did** — three or four lines on how the reported period came in
   against what the model previously carried, and against consensus where pulled.
   Actual against prior estimate, at the lines that moved most.
3. **What changed in the forecast, and why** — the re-forecast in words, not a cell
   list: which periods moved, in which direction, on whose basis. Where the current
   year rose and the out-years fell, say so and say why; that combination is common
   and looks like an error if unexplained.
4. **Corporate actions reflected**, or explicitly none.
5. **Where the model now sits against guidance and consensus** — the material
   divergences, each with its driver, distinguishing a real difference of view from
   a definition mismatch.
6. **What was not sourced** — blanks left blank, documents that could not be
   retrieved, anything awaiting the analyst.
7. **What needs the analyst's eye** — overrides that the new period may have made
   stale, and any judgement taken that they should confirm.

No rating, no target, no recommendation. It reports what the roll did.

**Never render an empty value.** No nulls, no unresolved placeholders, no orphaned label with
nothing behind it, no score against an uncomputed denominator. A row with no content is omitted
and named once in the sourcing note, never left blank or zeroed.

**Run the defaults, then say what else exists.** Only the blocking item above blocks; everything
else has a default and the default runs. Where the analyst supplies the subject and nothing more,
resolve the settings, state them in one line, and build. Do not hold the run to present a menu of
options — name in one line what else is available and carry on.
