# Synergy scale checks

Cost synergies look small or large depending on what they are measured against. Show both checks
side by side in Table E, and say which one the confidence read rests on.

- **% of combined cost base.** Use the company's figure if disclosed (deck, M&A call, later
  conferences, closing release). Otherwise compute it *(impl.)* = cost synergies ÷ combined operating
  costs (e.g. pro forma revenue − pre-synergy pro forma EBITDA), with the source of each input.
- **% of target EBITDA.** Cost synergies ÷ the target's EBITDA, stating the period (LTM or the
  forecast year the company used).

Ask call 2 for the combined cost base and for how management has sized the synergies in the deck, on
the M&A call, at later conferences and in the closing release.
