# Pre-synergy multiple and leverage

Companies often publish the pre-synergy multiple and pre-synergy leverage only as chart labels on
the investor presentation's valuation slide, not in the release or 8-K text.

1. **Slide follow-up.** If the pre-synergy / as-paid multiple is missing after the first calls, make
   one follow-up call naming the slides, not the deck in general: "the transaction valuation and pro
   forma leverage slides of <acquirer>'s investor presentation on <target>, including web copies of
   the deck: TEV / EBITDA before and after synergies, and net debt / EBITDA before and after
   synergies at close".
2. **Back-out, only if the follow-up finds nothing.** Pre-synergy multiple =
   `EV ÷ (EV ÷ headline multiple − synergies)`; note if synergies are a floor. Label it
   *(impl.; company figure not found)* and note that it assumes the headline EV and synergy amount
   match the company's own inputs. Same for pre-synergy leverage.
3. **A company figure always replaces an implied one.** If both turn up, show the company figure.
