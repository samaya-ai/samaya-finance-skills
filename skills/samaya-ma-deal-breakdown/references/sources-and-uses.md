# Table C — sources & uses

Read before building Table C. Applies to every deal; the closed-deal rules apply once the deal has
closed.

## Call 1b — closing cash flows (closed deals only)

Run alongside calls 1–4 once the deal is known to have closed, in a fresh session:

name the documents — the closing 8-K pro forma balance sheet cash adjustments, the financing 8-Ks
and the closing release. Ask for: equity raised at close with the price and share count at close
(and any collar or price adjustment); every debt tranche as named in the filing with its dollar
amount, including the dollar equivalent of any foreign-currency piece; cash proceeds net of costs;
each cash use (holders, awards, debt repaid, notes bought back in tenders vs exchanged, facilities
terminated, fees, change in cash) and the date each payment was made. For each pro forma cash
adjustment, its note reference and description.

If Table C's unexplained gap exceeds 5% of the total, make one follow-up call naming the missing
items and the document that should hold them.

## Rules

- **Basis label:** *as committed at signing* or *as funded at close*. Closed deal → as funded at
  close; the signing version goes in a footnote only. Never mix the two in one table.
- **Closed deals: build uses from cash flows.** Take uses from the cash adjustments in the closing
  8-K pro forma balance sheet (or financing 8-K), then tie them to the purchase-consideration table.
  Purchase consideration covers only payments to target holders; debt tender offers, refinancings
  and costs appear only in the cash adjustments.
- **Build each side from its own data.** Sources = everything raised; uses = everything paid.
  Never set one side equal to the other. Any gap goes on the uses side as *unexplained (impl.)*.
- **Sources as the filing names them:** each tranche (e.g. first-lien notes, second-lien notes,
  TLA, TLB) with its own amount, not a pricing-release aggregate.
- **Equity priced at close.** If the equity price had a collar or adjustment, show the closing price
  and share count; the signing price goes in the footnote.
- **Cash to holders** = basic shares tendered × price + ticking fee. Equity awards are a separate
  line. The diluted count belongs in Table A, not here.
- **Date every use.** A payment made before close (e.g. a break fee paid at signing) goes in a
  line below the table, *paid before close*, not in closing uses.
- **Every currency in the total.** Use the filing's own dollar figure first; only if it has none,
  convert at a dated rate. Show the original amount beside it. Never exclude a tranche from the
total.
- **Debt exchanges:** for any exchange or consent offer, ask how much was swapped for new notes
  (non-cash) and how much was bought back for cash; the cash part is a use.
- **Gaps:** label *unexplained (impl.)* with its formula. A named line needs a source — never name a
  plug with a guess. A gap over 5% of the total triggers the follow-up call above; if it stays over
  5%, say so in the takeaway line of Table C.
- **Undescribed filing lines:** if an amount matches an item found in another source (a facility
  terminated, a payoff, a fee), label it *matches <item> (same amount, <source>)*; otherwise
  *undescribed, note <ref>*. Match only on the same amount, never on a near one.

## Checklist

- [ ] C sources = uses; labelled *as committed at signing* or *as funded at close*.
- [ ] Each side built from its own data; tranches named as filed; equity at the closing price.
- [ ] All currencies in the total; uses dated; pre-close payments outside the table.
- [ ] Any gap labelled *unexplained (impl.)* with formula, under 5% of the total or broken out.
- [ ] Closed deals tie to the closing 8-K cash bridge.
