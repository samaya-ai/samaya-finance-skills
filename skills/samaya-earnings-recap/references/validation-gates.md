## VALIDATION GATES

Plus the common gates in `references/samaya-conventions.md` and the page-one gates in
`references/document-architecture.md`.

- Focus items established from evidence, stated in one line near the top, and led on by every
  default section.
- The takeaway's first sentence could not have been written from the release alone.
- Focus-item table on page one with reported, consensus and guide side by side and the difference
  computed in its own column; narrative shift table on page two with verbatim cells, a shift
  column and an implication column, and a row present for every focus item including unchanged
  ones.
- Report within the six-page ceiling excluding appendix; method, transcript basis, coverage and
  the not-run list all in the appendix and none of it above the body.
- Materiality pass run: the two or three most material findings sit on page one, no paragraph
  carries a comparison or more than three figures that should be a table, and the headline was
  rewritten last.
- The six-section template is followed in order, with nothing added and nothing dropped; sections
  with nothing to report still appear and say so.
- Consensus provider named at kickoff with no default applied, printed in the consensus column
  header and the event strip with its vintage date, and used for every comparison in the run.
- No section or table split across a page break; any table that breaks repeats its header row.
- Key numbers table carries percentages and margins rather than currency actuals, with reported
  and guidance as separate blocks and the previous guide shown under the new one.
- Any sentence above the key numbers table accounts for every headline line including those that
  went the other way, and claims no comparison the table does not show.
- Both charts present where the data supports them, drawn from the retrieved figures, each with a
  takeaway above and the same figures in a table underneath.
- Narrative section split positives and negatives, ordered by materiality, not forced into
  categories, with a two-sentence takeaway above that carries the section.
- Questions for management capped at five.
- Sources numbered with superscript markers on section headings; no hyperlinked sentences.
- Page one is one page: title over no more than two lines, one bold line under it, the five-item
  run strip in small type, the three-sentence takeaway, the what-changed table and the key-numbers
  table.
- The takeaway is present on every run regardless of company, sector or how thin the quarter was.
- The bold line under the title is not repeated by any row or bullet below it.
- The what-changed table carries a dated next-test column in every row.
- No prose introduces the key-numbers table; direction is carried by colour with a one-line key;
  differences in basis points for margins and growth rates; lines with no consensus marked not
  available rather than dropped.
- Takeaway is three sentences of plain text, names which line is the story, uses signs rather than
  the word minus, carries no em dashes and no beat or miss wording where the table shows the
  difference, and reads as a message rather than a document when read aloud.
- Negatives in the report carry a minus sign, in tables and in prose.
- Key debates ordered from answered most fully to least, handling labelled only as answered,
  partial or not answered, brokers named by firm and never individuals.
- At most one callout per page, labelled by what it says.
- Nothing addresses a reader or a listener; no glossary or definition block anywhere.
- Anyone stopping after page one would leave with the finding.
- Every heading idiomatic English, and every sub-heading naming its subject and its metric rather
  than a topic alone.
- Headline states a finding about the business, not a circumstance of the event.
- Event line on page one is one or two sentences, not a block; transcript basis and quality in the
  appendix.
- No table that restates the release without adding to it.
- Every expected headline figure either present or marked with the retrieval routes that were
  tried; no blank cells.
- Report within the ten-page ceiling excluding appendix.

- The default set ran without a blocking menu; only elected sections built where a subset
  was named.
- Samaya called directly as the first action.
- Masthead, headline, provenance strip and takeaway all on page one, in that order; the
  headline states a finding, not a subject.
- The provenance strip is four labelled lines and no more; everything further is in the appendix.
- The takeaway carries all four labelled parts and sits on page one — never merged into the
  conclusion, never at the end of the document.
- Nothing reporting an absence appears above the body. No empty value rendered anywhere.
- Authorship reads Samaya AI in properties, footers and any notes; no personal name anywhere.
  Styling taken from samaya-brand, not restated locally.
- Prepared remarks and question and answer kept as separate corpora throughout.
- Every answer score stated as answered, partially answered, deflected or
  declined, with the question quoted where not answered.
- Theme counts given as counts of separate questioners.
- Guidance carried as words, any derived figure labelled and its arithmetic shown.
- No outside individual named anywhere.
- Every retrieved figure and quotation cited at the point of use with a working
  link, or attributed by file name where uploaded.
- Gaps named, nothing estimated.
- The release is reported in the numbers section and release-sourced findings are
  labelled as such; the call's contribution is stated as the delta.
- The trailing four-period table is present — revenue, margin, EPS and growth — and
  tone is read against the trailing three-period baseline, not only the prior call.
- Segment driver splits are given only where disclosed; volume is never inferred
  from revenue and price unless both are given for the identical period.
- What to watch next is present: dated catalysts mapped to the thesis, named
  sensitivities, channel and order-book commentary, and three to six questions for
  management, the count following the material rather than a target.
- Inflection points flagged only where a dated change in direction is evidenced.
- Five-sentence conclusion present, leading with what the call changed.
