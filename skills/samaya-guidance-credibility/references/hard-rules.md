# Hard rules

Moved out of `SKILL.md` to keep it lean; read it before the first retrieval.

## HARD RULES — each of these has broken a real analysis

1. **Basis matching.** Guidance given at constant FX is scored against constant-FX delivery, not
   reported. Organic versus reported, pre- versus post-IFRS16, underlying versus statutory —
   match or do not score. State the basis in every row.
2. **Restatement flag.** If the company restated or rebased mid-window, the series is not
   comparable unless rebased. Flag it, rebase where the restated history is disclosed, and where
   it is not, break the table in two with a visible note. Never quietly compare across a
   restatement.
3. **Perimeter changes.** Material M&A or disposal inside a guided year changes the base. Note it
   against the affected rows; a "beat" delivered by an acquisition closing early is not a
   forecasting beat.
4. **Consensus as-of discipline.** Consensus must be the vintage **at the time** — the
   pre-print or pre-guidance mean, not today's number. Never back-fill with current data.
   Retrieve vintages in this order: (a) a dated consensus figure from the feed if the vintage is
   available; (b) **a dated broker note stating where consensus sat at that date** — a February
   2023 note reporting the Street's position is a legitimate vintage and is usually the most
   reachable source; (c) a company-compiled consensus published at the time. Where none is
   retrievable, leave the cell blank and say so. Blank cells are listed at the close so the
   reader can see which events lack a comparison.
5. **Metric definition discipline.** Use the market-primary variant (contributor count plus the
   issuer's own reporting convention). Where a metric has competing definitions, name the one in
   use.
6. **Interim derivation.** Where only FY and 1H are disclosed, derive 2H and label it derived.
   Never present a derived figure as reported.
7. **Withdrawn guidance is data, not a gap.** Suspension during a shock, and whether they
   reinstated early or late relative to peers, is evidence about the team.
8. **Tenure attribution.** Each guidance event is attributed to the CEO and CFO in post on the
   date it was issued. A track record inherited from a predecessor is not this team's record —
   this rule is what makes section 6 possible and it applies whether or not section 6 is run.
9. **Corporate history requires primary sources.** Perimeter events, acquisitions, disposals and
   management changes are stated only where they can be sourced to a filing, release, transcript
   or company announcement. Third-party aggregator and content-farm pages surfaced through
   retrieval do not support a factual claim about corporate history, however plausible. If it
   cannot be primary-sourced, **cut it** rather than including it with a caveat — a flagged
   unreliable fact still reads as a fact.
10. **Behavioural inference is permitted; forecasting is not.** You may state what this
   management team's record implies about how the current guide is likely to be handled — that a
   pre-flagged low end is where this company historically lands, or that a reset will land at
   current trading. Label it as inference from the record. You may not forecast the next
   reported result, issue a rating, or set a target.
11. **No fabrication.** A period without retrievable guidance stays blank with a flag. Absence of
   guidance is a finding; invented guidance is a build failure.
12. **The verdict reconciles against the consensus finding.** Where the consensus positioning
   section found that the guide predicted the outcome better than the Street did, that is direct
   evidence of guidance quality and the verdict must address it in terms. The report cannot
   conclude that guidance is unreliable while its own consensus section shows the guide beat
   consensus. Where both readings survive the evidence, the verdict says what reconciles them —
   most often that the guide was directionally right but repeatedly rebased, or accurate on one
   metric and not others — and names which. An unreconciled contradiction between the two is a
   build failure, not a nuance, and is the single most likely way this report is wrong.
