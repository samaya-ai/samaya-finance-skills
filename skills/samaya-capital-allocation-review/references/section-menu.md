# Section menu, status taxonomy and sub-section index

Moved out of `SKILL.md` to keep it lean; read it at kickoff, before presenting the menu.

## Contents

- SECTION MENU
- STATUS TAXONOMY
- SUB-SECTION INDEX

## SECTION MENU — presented every run, and answered before any retrieval

**Run the defaults, then say what else exists.** Where the invocation resolves a subject,
run the default sections immediately — do not hold the run to present the menu. Print one
line above the body (`Default set: <sections>. <n> optional sections available, listed in
the appendix.`) and build.

Present the menu and wait only where the subject or period cannot be resolved, the request is
ambiguous between this skill and a named neighbour, the run is expensive (a window beyond the
default, more than one name, Full depth), or the user asks what the skill can do.

Where the user names a section or sub-section, build exactly that and nothing else. Do not add
sections back for completeness, do not append omitted sections, and do not widen a single-item
request into its parent section.
**Default (1–5):**
1. **Coverage statement** — scope, window, activity types included, size floor, and what could
   not be established.
2. **Activity log** — every event, dated, typed, sized where disclosed, with confirmed or
   reported status and a source per row.
3. **Deal economics and stated rationale** — consideration, multiples paid where disclosed, funding
   mix, and the rationale as management themselves put it, retrieved from the announcement, the
   presentation and the call. Where the rationale given at announcement differs from how the same
   transaction is described later, both are shown with their dates.
4. **Live and pending** — announced but not completed, subject to conditions, under regulatory
   review, or reported and unconfirmed, with expected timing where stated.
5. **Pattern read** — what the activity says about the name or the sector: consolidation phase,
   portfolio reshaping, balance sheet repair, capital return preference.

**Optional (6–19):**
6. Buyback execution detail — authorised against completed, pace, average price where disclosed
7. Stated capital allocation framework over time — how management describe their priorities in
   presentations and on calls, and whether the wording has moved
8. Capital raise detail — instrument, terms, discount, use of proceeds, dilution
9. Stake changes and activist positions — holder, size, dates, stated intentions
10. Regulatory and antitrust status of pending transactions
11. Financing structure of announced deals, including bridge and take-out arrangements
12. Sector multiple reference table built from disclosed deal metrics
13. Acquirer and target profile analysis across the sector
14. Cross-border and inbound activity
15. Sponsor and private capital participation
16. Break fees, conditions and termination provisions where disclosed
17. Post-announcement share price reaction per event
18. Per-name detail (coverage list and sector scopes)
19. **Allocation pattern and execution** — what the recorded activity says about the team's
    allocation discipline, from confirmed activity only and built where figures are disclosed:
    multiples paid in context, buyback execution, reversals and round trips, and cadence. Runs
    after the activity log and before the conclusion.

## STATUS TAXONOMY — fixed, and stated in the output

Every entry carries exactly one:

- **Completed** — closed and effective.
- **Announced** — formally announced by a party, not yet closed. Conditions noted.
- **Terminated** — announced and subsequently abandoned, lapsed or blocked. Reason where stated.
- **Reported** — press or third-party reported, not confirmed by any party. Outlet and date
  named. This category never carries an inferred value, multiple or timeline.
- **Denied** — reported and subsequently denied by a party. Both the report and the denial dated.

Reported and Denied entries appear in a visually separate block, never interleaved with confirmed
activity in the same table.

## SUB-SECTION INDEX — every item below is individually selectable

Any single item may be requested on its own. A user who wants only the buyback execution detail
gets that and nothing else — no surrounding sections, no padding.

**1 Coverage statement** — 1a scope and window · 1b activity types and size floor · 1c categories
known to be under-captured

**2 Activity log** — 2a confirmed activity · 2b reported and unconfirmed, as its own block ·
2c terminated and denied

**3 Deal economics and stated rationale** — 3a consideration and multiples paid where disclosed ·
3b funding mix · 3c rationale as management stated it, by source and date · 3d changes in how a
transaction is described over time

**4 Live and pending** — 4a announced but not closed · 4b regulatory and conditional status ·
4c rumoured and unconfirmed approaches

**5 Pattern read** — 5a the pattern across the log · 5b an explicit no-pattern statement where the
log does not support one

**19 Allocation pattern and execution** (optional) — 19a multiples paid in context · 19b buyback
execution · 19c reversals and round trips · 19d cadence

Optional sections 6 to 19 are each individually selectable as listed above; section 19's four
components are individually selectable as listed here.
