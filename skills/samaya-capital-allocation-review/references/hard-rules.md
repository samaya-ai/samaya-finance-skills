# Hard rules

Moved out of `SKILL.md` to keep it lean; read it before the first retrieval.

## HARD RULES — each of these has broken a real tracker

1. **Status on every row.** No entry exists without one. A row whose status cannot be established
   does not go in the log.
2. **Never infer deal economics.** Where consideration or multiple is undisclosed, the cell reads
   undisclosed. Do not derive an implied multiple from press estimates and present it as the deal
   multiple, and do not carry a rumoured price into a table of confirmed transactions.
3. **Date properly, and distinguish which date.** Announcement, completion and effective dates
   differ, sometimes by a year. State which date each entry is dated on.
4. **Multiples need their basis.** A deal EV/EBITDA is meaningless without knowing whether it is
   trailing, forward, pre- or post-synergy, and pre- or post-IFRS 16. State the basis or state
   that the basis is undisclosed.
5. **Currency and as-of.** Deal values in their announced currency with the currency named. Where
   converted, state the rate date. Never silently convert.
6. **Repetition is not confirmation.** A rumour carried by several outlets is one rumour. Count
   sources, do not compound them into credibility.
7. **Coverage honesty.** Say what the window and sources could establish and what they could not.
   Smaller transactions and private-market activity are systematically under-captured, and a
   tracker that does not say so implies a completeness it does not have.
8. **The pattern read follows the log.** Draw the sector or company narrative from the events
   recorded, not from a prior view of what the sector is doing. If the log does not support a
   pattern, say there is no clear pattern.
9. **Buyback authorised is not buyback executed.** Track them as separate facts. An authorisation
   is a permission, not a purchase.
10. **No fabrication.** Do not populate a log from general knowledge of a sector's deal history,
    and do not assume a company has been active because its peers have. Nothing found is a
    finding.
