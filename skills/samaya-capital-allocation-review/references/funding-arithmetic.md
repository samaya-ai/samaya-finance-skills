# The quiet window and the funding arithmetic

Moved out of `SKILL.md` to keep it lean; read it before writing the headline, on every run.

## Contents

- THE QUIET WINDOW — the headline when no transaction dominates
- THE FUNDING ARITHMETIC — computed on every run

## THE QUIET WINDOW — the headline when no transaction dominates

The skill otherwise assumes a dominant event to lead on. Where there is not one, leading on the
absence — "no acquisitions this window" — is true and is not the finding. It describes the window
and misses what moved inside it.

**Where no single transaction dominates the window, the headline is what changed in the run rate.**
A buyback that halved, a dividend increase that slowed against its own history, a programme outlook
that moved from a firm figure to an upper limit — any of these is the finding.

**Before the headline is written, compare each recurring use of cash against the same period a year
earlier, and against the company's own stated plan.** Where a line moved materially, that line is
the candidate headline. The recurring uses to compare: the buyback run rate, the ordinary and any
special dividend, and any standing programme's outlook wording and figure.

## THE FUNDING ARITHMETIC — computed on every run, in the workbook and stated in the report

The skill otherwise records the cash figures and leaves them unconnected, so a reader has every
input and no conclusion. This connects them. It is description, not judgement: it adds no view on
whether the capital was well allocated. It runs on every scope — single name, or per name in a
sector read — and is present whether or not the optional allocation-pattern section was elected.

Four figures, each computed in the workbook and stated in the report:

- **Free cash flow after dividends**, against the capital returned or committed over the window.
- **The gap**, in the currency and as a share of free cash flow, with a line saying what covered
  it — cash, disposals, or new borrowing.
- **Net debt to EBITDA**, on a stated definition, at the start and the end of the window.
- **Where a programme has an announced total still to complete**, the remaining amount and what
  would fund it on current run rates.

**Against the stated target.** Where the company states a leverage target, set the computed net
debt to EBITDA against it. Where it states none, say so and compute the figure anyway, rather than
recording that no target was found.

Every figure carries its Samaya provenance link, and each computed figure cites the retrieved
inputs it was built from. Where an input is not disclosed, the figure names which input is missing
and is not estimated around.
