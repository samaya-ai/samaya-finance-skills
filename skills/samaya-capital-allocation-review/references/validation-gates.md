# Validation gates — before handing back

Moved out of `SKILL.md` to keep it lean; read it before handing back.

Plus the common gates in `references/samaya-conventions.md` and the page-one gates in
`references/document-architecture.md`.

- No sayings anywhere: no balanced clause pairs, no "X, not Y" in any position, no metaphors, no
  callout label describing the document, no phrase that sounds like meaning without carrying any.
  Headings, lead-ins, callout labels and table cells all checked.
- Ordinary words throughout; no academic vocabulary.
- Where an answer, a response or a disclosure is assessed, the verdict wording is neutral and the
  answerer is not graded.
- No section or table split across a page break; any table that breaks repeats its header row.
- Verdict line present at the top in the analyst voice, with signs rather than the word minus.
- Deployment chart present where the window covers three years or more, or its absence explained
  in one line.
- Prose above any table accounts for every line that matters, and claims no comparison the table
  does not show.
- Prose cited with superscript numbers to a numbered source list; no hyperlinked sentences; table
  rows keep their own source, date and link.
- Every comparison rendered as adjacent columns with a difference column — never as two tables or
  as consecutive paragraphs; no delta restated in words where the column already shows it; no
  blank cell where one side of a pair was not retrievable.
- The section menu was presented and the user's selection received before any retrieval began.
- Only the selected sections and sub-sections appear; nothing was added back for completeness.

- Coverage statement present, including under-captured categories.
- Every row carries a status, a date, a date type, a source, and the source class it came from.
- Confirmed and reported activity in separate blocks, never interleaved.
- No inferred consideration, multiple or timeline on any reported entry.
- Multiples carry their basis, or state that the basis is undisclosed.
- Currencies named; conversions dated or absent.
- Authorised and executed buybacks tracked separately.
- The pattern read cites only events present in the log above it.
- Nothing populated from general sector knowledge.
- Five-sentence conclusion present, drawn only from the log, with rumour never stated as
  confirmed and the coverage boundary closing it.
- Where the allocation pattern and execution section was run: confirmed activity only, every figure
  carries its provenance link, no multiple inferred from a transaction value, and no pattern read
  drawn where fewer than three transactions of a type fall in the window.
